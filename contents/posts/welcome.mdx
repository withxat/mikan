## A little space of your own

Welcome to **Mikan**, a personal blog with warm colors, a paper edge, and room to breathe.

A blog does not need a grand opening. Start with a thought, a small discovery, or something you made today.

> Small things, written down, become something worth keeping.

## Make it yours

Your site's name, introduction, links, and metadata live together in `src/site.config.ts`. Change them once, and the rest of the site follows.

- Write in Markdown or MDX.
- Keep unfinished posts private with `draft: true`.
- Share your work in the optional projects section.
- Let readers follow along through [RSS](/feed.xml).

Read the [writing guide](/post/writing-guide) to publish your first post.
